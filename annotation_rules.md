# Annotation Rules

Assign **exactly ONE label** per comment: the **primary communicative function**. Use the decision order below.

---

## Labels

| Label | Description | Indicators |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| **ADVICE** | Instructs OP to take action or gives concrete steps | "you should…", "try…", "talk to…", "file…", "break up…", "report…" |
| **WARNING** | Predicts negative consequences or advises against due to risk | "this will get worse", "you will regret it", "it won't end well", "that can ruin…" |
| **SUPPORT** | Comforts, reassures, validates, or empathizes | "I'm sorry", "you can get through this", "you're not alone", "that must be hard" |
| **OPINION** | Shares perspective, beliefs, or experiences (no advice/comfort) | "I think…", "In my experience…", "When this happened to me…" |
| **APPRAISAL** | Evaluates or judges without instructions or comfort | "that's toxic", "that was wrong", "he's manipulative", "this is unhealthy" |

---

## Decision Order (use first match)

1. **Does it tell the OP what to DO?** → ADVICE
2. **Does it mainly warn about negative consequences?** → WARNING
3. **Is it primarily emotional reassurance?** → SUPPORT
4. **Is the commenter sharing a perspective or experience?** → OPINION
5. **Otherwise (evaluation/judgment only)** → APPRAISAL

---

## Rules

- One label per comment. Choose the **dominant** function.
- Advice + comfort → **ADVICE**. Advice + risk prediction → **WARNING**.
- Ignore formatting, links, emojis, slang.
