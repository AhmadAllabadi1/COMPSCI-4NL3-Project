# Annotation Rules

Assign **exactly ONE label** per comment representing the **primary communicative function**.
Use the decision order below.

---

## Labels

| Label         | Description                                                                   | Indicators                                                                         |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ADVICE**    | Instructs OP to take a specific action or provides concrete steps             | "you should…", "try…", "talk to…", "file…", "break up…", "report…"                 |
| **WARNING**   | Predicts negative consequences or discourages an action due to risk          | "this will get worse", "you will regret it", "it won't end well", "that can ruin…" |
| **SUPPORT**   | Provides emotional reassurance, empathy, or encouragement                     | "I'm sorry", "you can get through this", "you're not alone", "that must be hard"   |
| **OPINION**   | Asks neutral questions or shares a perspective without judging or instructing | "why did…?", "what happened?", "I wonder…", "I feel like…"                         |
| **APPRAISAL** | Judges or evaluates a person, behavior, or situation                          | "that's toxic", "that was wrong", "he's manipulative", "this is unhealthy"        |

---

## Decision Order (apply first matching rule)

1. **Does it tell the OP what to DO?** → ADVICE
2. **Does it mainly warn about negative consequences?** → WARNING
3. **Is it emotional reassurance?** → SUPPORT
4. **Is it a neutral question or conversational perspective?** → OPINION
5. **Otherwise (judgment/evaluation/meta comment)** → APPRAISAL

---

## Additional Rules

* One label per comment; choose the **dominant function**.
* Advice + comfort → **ADVICE**
* Advice + risk prediction → **WARNING**
* "I think" + judgment → **APPRAISAL**
* Clarifying or conversational questions → **OPINION**
* Moderator or bot messages → **APPRAISAL**
* Ignore links, formatting, emojis, or slang.
